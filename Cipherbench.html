The numbers ▸
Measured values come off the Pico's own clock: 100 timed runs after 3 warm-up runs. est. values are calculated from the speed measured at a smaller size, because timing a big size 100 times would take hours to days (PRESENT at 100 KB in MicroPython is about 5 days). Speed stays almost flat as data grows, so these are close projections, not timed results. Ascon is the least certain. MicroPython 10 MB is 100× its 100 KB figure. Corrected three MicroPython 100 KB figures in the original report were off by about 1000× (a units slip) and are recalculated from the measured 1 KB rate. Credit CSIT321 Group 33 team project.
